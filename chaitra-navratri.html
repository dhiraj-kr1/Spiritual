About Chaitra Navratri

Chaitra Navratri arrives during the auspicious vernal equinox, marking both the cosmic beginning of creation and the moral renewal of human consciousness.

Falling in the bright fortnight of Chaitra (March–April), Chaitra Navratri — the Vasantik Navratri — holds first among the four Navratris of the Hindu calendar. Ancient scriptures confirm this is the moment of creation itself: the day Brahma commenced the creation of the cosmos on the very first day of Chaitra Shukla Pratipada, making Navratri's very first moment the birth of time and dharmic order.

In Ayodhya Dham, Chaitra Navratri is observed with rare intensity. It is not only Shakti sadhana — it is the spiritual preparation for Ram Navami, the birth of Bhagwan Shri Ram. Ayodhya devotees observe these nine days as a combined adoration of both Devi Shakti and Maryada Purushottam — understanding that Shri Ram himself is born of divine Shakti's grace and the eternal dance of cosmic dharma.

"Yaa Devi Sarva-bhuteshu Shakti Rupena Sansthita — O Goddess, who abides in all beings in the form of Power — we offer you our profound salutation, again and again."

For Ayodhya devotees, Chaitra Navratri's culmination on Ram Navami renders it a uniquely blessed festival — nine days where Shakti and Bhakti merge as one indivisible stream of devotion. The ancient Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, and the Ram Janmbhoomi Mandir radiate with an unbroken atmosphere of divine reverence throughout all nine sacred days.

Ghatasthapana: The Cosmic Invocation

The primordial Nupura performed on Pratipada Day (1st tithi) and noted inside Devi Durga's ten sacred Sanskrit ślokas and Sapta Dharas.

Ghatasthapana Shuddhi Muhurta & Protocol

पूजा के लिए जाएं
①
Mitti Ki Bell (Earth ✦ Ghat)
Sacred Clay Vessel

A vessel of sacred clay — the Ghata — is consecrated and placed on the altar on the pious Pratipada morning. The Ghata represents the primordial womb of creation and the dwelling place of Devi.

②
Kalash Sthapana
Kalash Consecration

They sow sacred seeds — barley or wheat — in consecrated soil inside the Kalash. As the barley sprouts over nine days, it mirrors the growth of divine blessings and is a living symbol of fertility and abundance.

③
Panch-Pallav & Coconut
Panch-Pallav Installation

Five sacred leaves — mango, peepal, banyan, gular, and ashoka — are placed in the Kalash with a coconut representing the divine Goddess. The coconut is wrapped in a red cloth to invoke the fierce and protective form of the Mother.

④
Devi Avahana & Sankalpa
Inviting the Goddess

Devotees chant the Ghatasthapana mantra with great devotion and take the Sankalpa — a sacred vow to observe all nine days with purity of mind, fasting, and continuous worship, beseeching Devi to enter and reside within the sacred vessel.

✦ नव दुर्गा • Nav Durga ✦

Nav Durga — The Nine Cosmic Forms

Each day has at its root a Navaratri manifestation, each goddess endowed with great regional stories, spiritual roles, planetary blessings, and cosmic affinities.

Day 1 • Pratipada
Mountain Moon
माँ शैलपुत्री

Maa Shailputri

✦ The Daughter of the Himalayas

Rising eternal Parvati, residing in Muladhara Chakra. She holds a trident and lotus, rides a bull Nandi. Her worship grounds the devotee in nature's primal energy and bestows exceptional fortune and fearlessness.

Navaratna Dosha → Planet: Moon
Day 2 • Dvitiya
Tapasya Mars
माँ ब्रह्मचारिणी

Maa Brahmacharini

✦ The Ascetic Goddess of Tapas

Embodying supreme austerity, she walks barefoot carrying a rosary and water pot — depicting Sati's unflinching dedication before receiving Shiva as husband. Worshipping her disciplines the mind, purifies consciousness, and grants liberation from attachments.

Tapasya & Moksha → Planet: Mars
Day 3 • Tritiya
Warrior Venus
माँ चन्द्रघण्टा

Maa Chandraghanta

✦ The Radiance of the Crescent Bell

Adorned with a half-moon shaped like a bell on her forehead, she embodies cosmic beauty and warrior grace. Her ten-armed form rides a tiger, bestowing courage, bravery, and an aura of divine light upon her devotees.

Courage & Grace → Planet: Venus
Day 4 • Chaturthi
Sun Creation
माँ कूष्माण्डा

Maa Kushmanda

✦ The Creator of the Cosmic Egg

With the power of her smile, she created the universe — the primordial "Anda" or cosmic egg — dispelling the void of darkness. She is the solar goddess residing in the heart of the sun, bestowing radiance, vitality, and longevity to her devotees.

Solar Vitality → Planet: Sun
Day 5 • Panchami
Mother Mercury
माँ स्कन्दमाता

Maa Skandamata

✦ Mother of Skanda (Kartikeya)

Seated upon a lotus, she holds the infant Kartikeya in her lap — the divine general of the gods who would vanquish the demon Tarakasura. She bestows wisdom, salvation, and the unique grace of divine motherhood upon all sincere devotees.

Wisdom & Salvation → Planet: Mercury
Day 6 • Shashthi
Warrior Jupiter
माँ कात्यायनी

Maa Katyayani

✦ The Warrior Daughter (Mahishasur Mardini)

Born to sage Katyayana to slay Mahishasura, she is the most venerated form of the goddess — worshipped by the Gopis of Vrindavan for Shri Krishna as their husband. She bestows bravery, righteous action, and removes all obstacles from the devotee's path.

Shakti & Prem → Planet: Jupiter
Day 7 • Saptami
Fierce Saturn
माँ कालरात्रि

Maa Kalaratri

✦ The Annihilation of Darkness

Dark as night herself, her flowing hair and three glowing eyes blaze with a fierce radiance. Despite her terrifying appearance, she is called "Shubhankari" — the auspicious one — for she destroys all darkness, evil forces, and planetary afflictions for her devotees.

Inquiry: Maa Kalaratri → Planet: Saturn
Day 8 • Ashtami
Purity Rahu
माँ महागौरी

Maa Mahagauri

✦ The Radiance of Purity

Luminous white as the moon and the jasmine flower, she represents perfect purity restored. After years of deep tapasya for Shiva, the river Ganga cleansed her, restoring her golden-white brilliance. She absolves all past sins and purifies the entire being of her devotees.

Purity & Absolution → Planet: Rahu
Day 9 • Navami
Siddhi Ketu
माँ सिद्धिदात्री

Maa Siddhidatri

✦ Bestower of Supernatural Wisdom

Seated on a fully blossomed lotus and worshipped by gods, sages, Siddhas, and Gandharvas alike, she grants all eight Siddhis — the supernatural powers — and all kinds of Niddhis, supreme moksha, and the direct experience of transcendent divine consciousness.

Siddhi & Mukti → Planet: Ketu

Daily Puja Vidhi & Timetable

A systematic devotional practice performed at prescribed times to invoke the presence of the Mother in the morning, afternoon, and evening.

Pratah Puja & Abhishek

⏱ Morning (04:00 – 07:00)
  • Ritualistic bathing and wearing clean white or red garments before worship.
  • Performing Kalash Abhishek with Gangajal, milk, and panchamrit on the Ghata
  • Offering fresh flowers, red chunari, and kumkum to the installed Devi image
  • Recitation of the prescribed Durga Saptashati chapter for the day
  • Offering Mishri, seasonal fruit and coconut as Bhog

Madhyanha Puja & Stotra

⏱ Midday (11:00 – 14:00)
  • Navarna Mantra Japa: 108 repetitions of the nine-syllable beej mantra
  • Recitation of Aparajita Stotra and Kavach for full divine protection
  • Navarna Homam at special fire-pits maintained in the major Devi temples
  • Sattvic fasting lunch: sabudana, fruits, sendha namak preparations
  • Bhagwati Jagran reading & Devi Mahatmya recitation

Sandhya Aarti & Maha Bhog

⏱ Sandhya (18:00 – 21:00)
  • Sandhya Deepam — lighting 9 sacred ghee lamps before Devi at dusk
  • Aarati with shankha, ghanta, and dhoop in prescribed ritual sequence
  • Recitation of Aarti: "Jai Ambe Gauri" and "Om Jai Jagdambe Mata"
  • Anushthana Maha Bhog distribution as Prasad to assembled devotees
  • All-night Jagran sessions with Mata ki Chowki on selected Navratri nights

✦ The Maha Culmination ✦

Maha Ashtami & Maha Navami

The two pinnacle days where all Navratri energy reaches transformative convergence — and Maryada Purushottam Shri Ram is born.

✦ Day 8 • Ashtami • Kanya Pujan & Havan

The Worship of Divine Daughters

  • Maha Ashtami celebrates the play of Maa Durga in Ayodhya. In Ayodhya, this becomes the formless Nirguna, alongside nine specially selected Bhavana.
  • Performing traditional Kanya Pujan of nine pre-pubescent girls representing nine forms of Devi
  • Offering traditional Halwa, Puri, and Kala Chana as the sacred Bhog to the divine daughters
  • Grand Maha Havan (sacred fire ceremony) conducted in Ayodhya's major Devi temples
✦ Day 9 • Navami • The Cosmic Advent of Shri Ram

The Cosmic Advent of Shri Ram

  • Ram Navami, falling on the 9th day of Chaitra Navratri, marks the divine birth of Bhagwan Shri Ram at the golden midday sextile in Kanak Bhawan and Janmbhoomi Mandir
  • Celebratory Abhishek of Ram Lalla at precise birth moment and grand Ram Janmotsav procession
  • Concluding Prasad Vitaran, Navami Aarti and Parana

Sacred Prasad of Chaitra Navratri

Traditional specific foods ceremonially prepared and offered to the Goddess, then distributed as Mahalaxmi Prasad.

Day 1–3 • Navratri Prasad

Sanji Ka Halwa & Poori

Traditional wheat-flour Halwa prepared with pure ghee and sugar, offered alongside Puri and Kala Chana — the three sacred offerings that form the complete Navratri Bhog.

Day 4–6 • Fasting Prasad

Sukha Kela Chana

Dried banana, roasted whole black chickpeas, and coconut pieces — a simple and deeply sattvic Prasad offered during the strict fasting days of the midpoint of Navratri.

Day 7–8 • Vrat Prasad

Kuttu & Singhara Khichdi

Buckwheat and water chestnut flour preparations — specifically permitted during Navratri vrat observance — offered with rock salt, potatoes, and pure ghee.

Day 9 • Ram Navami Prasad

Panchamrit & Kheer

On Ram Navami, Panchamrit — five sacred liquids mixed in the Abhishek — is distributed as Prasad, along with sweet Kheer prepared with pure milk, rice, and saffron blessed in the name of both Devi and Bhagwan Ram.
