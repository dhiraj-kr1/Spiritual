Saryu Ghat, Ayodhya:
Where the Sacred River
Meets the Eternal Ram

The Saryu is not just a river — it is the living memory of Ayodhya. Along its ghats, Bhagwan Shri Ram was born, ruled, loved, and ultimately returned to the divine through Jalasamadhi. Every ripple carries a prayer. Every dawn here is a darshan.

01 — SACRED SIGNIFICANCE

Why the Saryu is Unlike Any Other River

In the Valmiki Ramayana and the Ramcharitmanas, the Saryu is not merely a geographic feature — she is a divine witness to the entire leela of Shri Ram, from birth to the final return to Vaikuntha.

सरयू पावन पुलिन मनोहर। राम कीन्ह जहँ जलक्रीड़ा बहु बर॥

Sarayū pāvan pulin manohar. Rāma kīnha jahaṁ jalakrīḍā bahu bara.

The sacred, beautiful bank of the Saryu — where Shri Ram performed many delightful water-sports in his divine childhood.

— Ramcharitmanas, Bala Kanda

child_care

Ram's Childhood Playground

Young Ram, Lakshman, Bharata, and Shatrughna played along these very banks. The Saryu saw their first steps, their childhood games, and their training under Maharishi Vishwamitra.

self_improvement

Gateway to Moksha

The scriptures declare the Saryu as Mokshadayini — the giver of liberation. A holy dip here, especially on auspicious days, is believed to wash away the accumulated karma of many lifetimes.

waves

Site of Ram's Jalasamadhi

According to the Uttara Kanda, Bhagwan Ram concluded his earthly leela by walking into the Saryu with his entire kingdom — entering the divine waters and ascending to Vaikuntha. The spot is known as Guptar Ghat.

02 — THE SACRED GHATS

Five Ghats Every Pilgrim Should Know

The Saryu waterfront stretches for several kilometres, lined with ghats each carrying its own sacred history and ritual purpose.

water

Ram Ki Paidi (Naya Ghat)

Most Visited

The grand renovated ghat built in the 1980s, featuring wide stone steps descending directly to the Saryu. This is where the magnificent Saryu Aarti takes place every evening — rows of priests with large lamps, conch blowing, and thousands of devotees releasing diyas on the water. The atmosphere rivals Varanasi's Ganga Aarti in beauty and devotion.

🕯️ Evening Aarti 🌅 Sunrise Snan 🪔 Diya Visarjan
self_improvement

Guptar Ghat

Most Sacred

Located slightly downstream, Guptar Ghat ("the hidden ghat") is the holiest spot on the Saryu — traditionally identified as the site where Bhagwan Ram took Jalasamadhi and returned to his divine abode. The Chakraharji Vishnu temple stands here, and the atmosphere is profoundly still. Many saints choose this ghat for meditation and last rites.

🙏 Jalasamadhi Site 🕌 Chakraharji Temple 🧘 Meditation & Silence
local_fire_department

Swarg Dwar Ghat

Literally the "Gateway to Heaven," this is the primary cremation ghat of Ayodhya. It is considered supremely auspicious to have one's last rites performed here, as the Saryu is believed to grant liberation (moksha) to all who depart from her banks. A final dip for the dying is considered the ultimate grace.

🔥 Antyesti Rituals 🕊️ Moksha Sthal
spa

Lakshman Ghat

Dedicated to Lakshman — the ideal younger brother and the very symbol of selfless service. This quieter ghat is associated with the Lakshmana Kila (Lakshman's fort). Pilgrims come here to pray for family bonds, siblings, and the virtue of seva (selfless service). The Saryu here flows with a particular gentleness.

🏰 Lakshmana Kila 🤝 Family Prayers
sunny

Brahmakund Ghat

An ancient sacred kund (pool) fed by the Saryu, considered especially potent for ritual bathing on Ram Navami and Kartik Purnima. Local tradition holds that Lord Brahma himself bathed here, and the waters carry an exceptional sanctity for ancestral offerings (pitru tarpan).

🌕 Kartik Purnima 🙏 Pitru Tarpan
03 — THE EVENING AARTI

Saryu Aarti — Light Meeting Water

Every evening as the sun dips below Ayodhya's skyline, the ghats come alive with the sound of conches, bells, and devotional hymns as priests perform the grand Saryu Aarti at Ram Ki Paidi.

ॐ सरयू मातर्नमोस्तु ते।
मोक्षदायिनि पावनि नमस्ते॥

O Mother Saryu, we bow to you — O Purifier, O Bestower of Moksha, we salute you.

04 — PILGRIM'S GUIDE

How to Experience Saryu Ghat

schedule

Best Time to Visit

  • ✦ Pre-dawn (4–6 AM) — the most spiritually potent time for a holy dip. Pilgrims chant Ram Naam as they enter the water.
  • ✦ Evening (6–8 PM) — arrive 30 minutes early for a good spot at the Aarti. The sight of thousands of diyas on the dark water is unforgettable.
  • ✦ October–March — the best weather season for Ayodhya overall.
checklist

What to Bring

  • check_circle Change of clothes for after your holy dip
  • check_circle Diyas (earthen lamps) and flowers for offering — also available at ghat stalls
  • check_circle Sesame seeds and black til for ancestral offerings (pitru tarpan)
  • check_circle Footwear that slips on/off easily — the steps can be wet
info

Etiquette & Tips

  • check_circle Maintain silence and reverence — this is a place of worship, not tourism
  • check_circle Do not use soap or shampoo in the Saryu — it pollutes the sacred water
  • check_circle Chant Jai Shri Ram or Om Namah Shivaya as you enter the water
  • check_circle During festivals, arrive very early — crowds can be enormous
05 — हिन्दी में

सरयू घाट — पूर्ण परिचय

हिन्दी

सरयू नदी — अयोध्या की प्राण-धारा

सरयू नदी केवल जल की धारा नहीं है — यह अयोध्या की आत्मा है। इसी पावन नदी के तट पर मर्यादा पुरुषोत्तम भगवान श्रीराम ने जन्म लिया, खेले, राज किया, और अंत में इसी के गर्भ में जलसमाधि लेकर वैकुण्ठ को प्रस्थान किया। सरयू के हर लहर में राम-नाम की गूँज है।

अयोध्या के घाट तट पर सुबह-सुबह श्रद्धालुओं की भीड़ उमड़ पड़ती है। ब्रह्ममुहूर्त में सरयू में स्नान करना अत्यंत पुण्यकारी माना जाता है। कहते हैं कि सरयू का एक डुबकी कई जन्मों के पापों को धो देती है — इसीलिए इसे मोक्षदायिनी कहा जाता है।

गुप्तार घाट वह पावन स्थान है जहाँ भगवान राम ने समस्त अयोध्यावासियों सहित जलसमाधि ली थी।

— उत्तरकाण्ड, वाल्मीकि रामायण

सायंकाल राम की पैड़ी पर होने वाली सरयू आरती देखने योग्य है। हजारों दीप जब सरयू की लहरों पर तैरते हैं और पुजारियों की घण्टियाँ आकाश को गुंजायमान करती हैं, तब लगता है मानो साक्षात् स्वर्ग धरती पर उतर आया हो। यह दृश्य वाराणसी की गंगा आरती से किसी भी प्रकार कम नहीं है।

तीर्थयात्री के लिए सरयू दर्शन का सबसे उत्तम समय है — प्रातःकाल ब्रह्ममुहूर्त में स्नान और सायंकाल आरती। पर्व के दिनों में — विशेष रूप से रामनवमी, कार्तिक पूर्णिमा और दीपावली पर — सरयू तट का नज़ारा अलौकिक हो जाता है।

सरयू तटे राघवः — सरयू के तट पर राघव विराजमान हैं।

At the banks of Saryu, Raghav (Ram) eternally abides.

Whether you come at the first blush of dawn for a silent holy dip, or in the golden evening to watch a thousand diyas set sail on the Saryu — you will leave with something you cannot name, but will carry forever. That is the gift of this river. That is the grace of Ram.
