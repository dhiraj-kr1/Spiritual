Maa Katyayani
Born to Slay Mahishasura

The sixth of the nine divine forms of Nav Durga - Katyayani, born from the combined radiance of all the gods to do what none of them could alone: destroy the demon Mahishasura. Four-armed, lion-riding, blazing with divine fire, she is the most formidable warrior form of the goddess - and yet the Gopis of Vrindavan worshipped her for love.

01 - IDENTITY

Who is Maa Katyayani?

She is named after the sage Katyayana, in whose ashram she was born - or according to some traditions, who performed intense penance to obtain her as his daughter. She is four-armed, golden-complexioned like blazing fire, lion-riding and ready for war.

auto_awesome
Born of All Gods
She was not born of any single parent - she emerged from the combined radiance of Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva and all the devas whose light fused into one blazing feminine form. She is the collective divine power made incarnate.
back_hand
Four Arms, Four Gifts
Her four arms hold: a lotus (purity), a sword (the sword gifted by Shiva), her right hands in abhaya mudra (protection) and varada mudra (boon-giving). Destruction and grace in equal measure.
brightness_7
Chakra: Ajna
Her worship activates the Ajna chakra - the third eye, the centre of intuition, discernment and divine wisdom. She destroys the demons of ignorance and confusion from within the mind itself.
favorite
The Gopis' Goddess
In the Bhagavata Purana, the Gopis of Vrindavan worshipped Katyayani for an entire month to obtain Shri Krishna as their divine companion - making her the bridge between the fierce Nav Durga tradition and Vaishnava devotion.

चन्द्रहासोज्जवलकरा शार्दूलवरवाहना।
कात्यायनी शुभं दद्यात् देवी दानवघातिनी॥

Candrahāsojjvalakarā śārdūlavara-vāhanā, Kātyāyanī śubhaṃ dadyāt devī dānava-ghātinī.

"She whose hand blazes with the moon-bright sword, who rides the finest of lions - may the goddess Katyayani, slayer of demons, bestow auspiciousness upon me." Her dhyana shloka is chanted on Day 6 to invoke her warrior energy and the power to overcome inner and outer obstacles.

- Devi Mahatmya · Navratri Dhyana Shloka

02 - THE BIRTH STORY

When All the Gods Became One

Mahishasura - the buffalo-demon - had terrorised the cosmos for aeons. He had conquered the heavens and driven the gods from their thrones. No single god could slay him. So all of them became one.

01

Mahishasura's Boon

The demon Mahishasura performed great penance and obtained a boon from Brahma: no man or god could slay him. He took this to mean he was immortal - not anticipating that the creator's boon said nothing about a woman. His arrogance became his doom.

02

The Gods Unite Their Radiance

The gods assembled in desperation. Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva, Indra, Agni, Vayu, Yama - each poured their divine energy outward. These streams of light fused into a single blinding radiance. From that cosmic fire, a woman emerged - beautiful, terrible, blazing - Maa Katyayani.

03

Armed by Every God

Each god gave her a weapon from their own arsenal: Shiva gave his trident, Vishnu his Sudarshana chakra, Indra his thunderbolt, Varuna his conch, Agni his flames, Vayu his bow - gift after gift, until she stood fully armed with the power of the entire universe.

04

The Slaying of Mahishasura

The battle was fierce and legendary - described in the Devi Mahatmya across multiple chapters. Mahishasura changed forms repeatedly trying to escape. Finally, with Shiva's sword and her own divine fury, Maa Katyayani severed his head and liberated the cosmos. The gods rained flowers from the heavens.

03 - THE GOPIS & KRISHNA

The Goddess Worshipped for Love

There is a second, deeply beloved story of Katyayani - this time not from the battlefield of Lanka or the heavens, but from the banks of the Yamuna in Vrindavan.

According to the Bhagavata Purana (Skandha 10), the Gopis - the young cowherd girls of Vrindavan - observed a month-long vrata in the month of Margashirsha, bathing in the Yamuna at dawn and worshipping Maa Katyayani's image made of sand on the riverbank.

Their prayer was singular and pure: "O Goddess Katyayani - Mahayogini, Maheshwari - make Nanda's son Krishna our husband."

Shri Krishna himself acknowledged this vrata, and eventually appeared to the Gopis in the divine Raas Leela. The Katyayani Vrat is still observed by unmarried girls praying for a good husband, connecting the fierce warrior goddess to the most tender form of devotion.

Gopi Mantra · Bhagavata Purana

कात्यायनि महामाये महायोगिन्यधीश्वरि।
नन्दगोपसुतं देवि पतिं मे कुरु ते नमः॥

"O Katyayani, great Maya, great Yogini, Maheshwari - O Devi, make the son of Nanda Gopa my husband. I bow to you."

- Bhagavata Purana 10.22.4

Beej Mantra · Day 6

ॐ कात्यायन्यै नमः

Om Kātyāyanyai Namah

Chant 108 times on Day 6. Her worship destroys enemies, removes obstacles to marriage, and activates the Ajna chakra. Unmarried devotees praying for a righteous partner receive her special blessing today.

04 - PUJA VIDHI

How to Worship Maa Katyayani on Day 6

01
Wear Red or Scarlet

Red is the colour of power, courage and divine fire - all of which Katyayani embodies. Wearing red on Day 6 honours her warrior nature and invites her strength and decisiveness into your life.

02
Offer Honey as Bhog

Honey (madhu) is the traditional offering for Maa Katyayani. Some traditions also offer paan (betel leaf). Offering honey is said to bring sweetness into relationships, remove obstacles in marriage and bless the devotee with prosperity.

03
Katyayani Vrat for Unmarried Girls

Following the Gopis' example, unmarried girls traditionally observe a fast on Day 6 and pray to Maa Katyayani for a devoted, righteous husband. The prayer should be made with a pure heart and complete surrender - as the Gopis surrendered to the goddess.

04
Chant Her Mantra 108 Times

With a rudraksha mala, chant ॐ कात्यायन्यै नमः 108 times. This activates the Ajna chakra - the third eye - and is said to destroy confusion, sharpen intuition and grant the devotee the courage to face any challenge.

05
Read Durga Saptashati Chapters 2–4

Day 6 aligns with the Madhyama Charitra of Devi Mahatmya - the central narrative of the goddess's great battle with Mahishasura. These chapters carry Katyayani's energy most directly and are ideal for reading or listening today.

05 - TEACHINGS

What Katyayani Teaches Us

groups

Unity Creates New Power

No single god could defeat Mahishasura. But when they combined their energies - surrendering their individual power for a collective purpose - something entirely new and greater was born. This is the teaching of cooperation over isolation.

visibility

Arrogance Has a Loophole

Mahishasura's boon protected him from all men - yet he was destroyed by a woman. His own arrogance and limited thinking created his downfall. Katyayani teaches us that no ego is truly invincible, and that what we dismiss is often what undoes us.

favorite

The Warrior Goddess Hears Love

The Gopis didn't approach the goddess with a weapon or a battle prayer. They came with love - wanting Krishna. And the same goddess who slew Mahishasura heard their tender prayer. The divine does not require fierceness to be approached; it only requires sincerity.

माँ कात्यायनी - देवताओं के तेज से जन्मी महिषासुर-मर्दिनी

नवरात्रि के षष्ठ दिन माँ कात्यायनी की पूजा होती है। महर्षि कात्यायन की घोर तपस्या से प्रसन्न होकर देवी ने उनके यहाँ पुत्री के रूप में जन्म लिया - इसीलिए इनका नाम कात्यायनी पड़ा। ये चतुर्भुजी हैं - एक हाथ में खड्ग (तलवार), दूसरे में कमल, दो हाथ अभय और वर मुद्रा में। सिंह इनका वाहन है और इनका रंग स्वर्णिम अग्निवर्ण है।

जब महिषासुर के अत्याचार से तीनों लोकों में त्राहि-त्राहि मच गई और कोई देव उसे न मार सका, तब समस्त देवताओं के तेज से एकत्रित होकर माँ कात्यायनी का अवतरण हुआ। प्रत्येक देवता ने अपना-अपना अस्त्र-शस्त्र माँ को अर्पित किया। माँ ने महिषासुर का वध करके देवताओं को उनका अधिकार वापस दिलाया।

भागवत पुराण के अनुसार मार्गशीर्ष मास में व्रज की गोपियों ने माँ कात्यायनी का व्रत रखकर उनसे नन्दकुमार श्रीकृष्ण को पति के रूप में माँगा। माँ ने उनकी मनोकामना पूरी की। आज भी कुंवारी कन्याएँ अच्छे वर की प्राप्ति के लिए इस दिन माँ कात्यायनी का व्रत रखती हैं।
