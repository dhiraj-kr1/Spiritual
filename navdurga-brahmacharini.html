Maa Brahmacharini
The Ascetic Goddess

The second of the nine divine forms of Nav Durga - Brahmacharini, the goddess of penance and unwavering devotion. She walks barefoot, her face luminous with the light of ten thousand years of tapasya, holding nothing but a japa mala and a kamandal. She is the supreme example of what devotion, discipline and love can achieve.

01 - IDENTITY

Who is Maa Brahmacharini?

"Brahma" here means tapasya - penance and sacred discipline. "Charini" means one who practises. She is Parvati in her form of intense austerity - the goddess who gave up everything for the sake of divine union with Shiva.

spa
Japa Mala
In her right hand she holds a rudraksha japa mala - the symbol of unbroken devotion, the tool of her ten-thousand-year chanting of Shiva's name.
water_drop
Kamandal
In her left hand she holds a kamandal - the ascetic's water pot - representing renunciation, simplicity, and the life of a true spiritual seeker.
directions_walk
No Vahana
Unlike most divine forms, Brahmacharini has no vehicle. She walks barefoot - a sign of her supreme humility, groundedness, and the hardship of the ascetic path she chose.
brightness_7
Chakra: Svadhishthana
Her worship activates the Svadhishthana chakra - the sacral chakra governing creativity, willpower and emotional endurance. She is the goddess of inner strength.

"She who holds in her lotus hands the rosary and the water-pot - may that supreme goddess Brahmacharini be pleased with me." This dhyana shloka is recited on Navratri Day 2 before beginning her puja and japa.

- Devi Mahatmya · Navratri Dhyana Shloka

02 - THE GREAT TAPASYA

Ten Thousand Years of Devotion

The story of Brahmacharini is the story of Parvati's tapasya - one of the most extraordinary acts of devotion in all of Hindu scripture.

First 1,000 Years

Living on Fallen Leaves

Parvati - born as Shailputri - resolved to win Lord Shiva as her husband, no matter the cost. For a thousand years she lived only on fallen leaves (parna), meditating in the forests, chanting Shiva's names, offering flowers and water at his unseen feet. This period earned her the name Aparna - one who does not even eat leaves.

Next 3,000 Years

Living Without Food

She abandoned even fallen leaves and subsisted only on water. She stood on one leg for years, her body thin as a reed, her mind one-pointed on Mahadeva. The gods trembled at the intensity of her penance. Himavan, her father, wept watching his daughter's self-denial and begged her to stop - she gently refused.

Thousands More Years

Living Without Water - Exposed to Elements

She gave up water itself. In summer she sat surrounded by five fires (panchagni tapasya). In the rains she stood in the open, drenched and still. In winter she sat in the Himalayan ice. Still she did not waver. Still she chanted "Om Namah Shivaya" with every breath. The cosmos shook. Even Brahma sent Narada to acknowledge her penance.

The Test

Shiva Comes in Disguise to Mock Her

Shiva, moved by her devotion yet wanting to test her resolve, came disguised as a young brahmin. He praised her beauty and mocked her choice of Shiva as a husband - "He is a wandering ascetic with snakes around his neck and ash-smeared skin. Why not choose a worthy king?" Parvati grew angry and ordered him to leave. At that moment Shiva revealed himself - her devotion had won.

03 - TEACHINGS

What Brahmacharini Teaches Us

psychology

Will is Stronger than Circumstance

Parvati was a princess of the Himalayas - she could have lived in comfort. She chose the hardest path. Her story teaches that the will to achieve something divine is more powerful than any obstacle the world places before us.

hourglass_bottom

Patience is a Form of Power

She did not complain when Shiva did not come quickly. She did not abandon her vow when her father begged her to. She understood that the deepest things take the longest time - and she was willing to give all the time she had.

self_improvement

Tapasya Transforms the Seeker

By the end of her tapasya, Parvati was no longer just the mountain king's daughter. She was the equal of Shiva himself - the full expression of Adi Shakti. Penance does not only win boons. It transforms the one who performs it.

Beej Mantra · Day 2

ॐ ब्रह्मचारिण्यै नमः

Om Brahmacharinyai Namah

Chant this mantra 108 times on Day 2 of Navratri. Her worship is said to bring endurance, willpower, purity of character and success in long endeavours.

04 - PUJA VIDHI

How to Worship Maa Brahmacharini on Day 2

01
Wear White or Orange

White represents purity and asceticism - the colour of her garments. Orange represents the sacred fire of tapasya. Either is auspicious on Day 2.

02
Offer Panchamrit & Sugar

Traditional bhog on Day 2 is sugar (shakkar) - offered to Maa Brahmacharini. It is said that offering sugar on this day blesses the devotee with long life and freedom from suffering. Panchamrit (milk, curd, ghee, honey, sugar) is also offered.

03
Maintain a Vrata - Even Partial Fast

In honour of her supreme tapasya, many devotees observe a fast or eat only fruits on Day 2. Even a partial fast - skipping one meal - is considered an act of devotion that resonates with her story.

04
Chant Her Mantra 108 Times

Use a rudraksha mala to chant ॐ ब्रह्मचारिण्यै नमः 108 times. This activates the Svadhishthana chakra and is said to build inner resilience, patience and clarity of purpose.

05
Read Durga Saptashati Chapters 2–4

Day 2 of Navratri corresponds to chapters 2–4 of the Devi Mahatmya - the story of Mahishasura's battle with the goddess. Reading or listening to these chapters while meditating on Brahmacharini is considered highly auspicious.

माँ ब्रह्मचारिणी - तप, त्याग और अनन्य भक्ति की देवी

नवरात्रि के द्वितीय दिन माँ ब्रह्मचारिणी की पूजा होती है। "ब्रह्म" का अर्थ है तपस्या और "चारिणी" का अर्थ है आचरण करने वाली - अर्थात् तपस्या का आचरण करने वाली देवी। ये सफेद वस्त्र धारण किए, नंगे पैर चलती हैं और हाथों में जपमाला तथा कमण्डल धारण करती हैं।

पूर्वजन्म में ये शैलपुत्री के रूप में हिमवान की पुत्री थीं। भगवान शिव को पति के रूप में प्राप्त करने के लिए इन्होंने अनेक सहस्र वर्षों तक घोर तपस्या की। पहले एक हजार वर्ष केवल फल-फूल पर रहीं, फिर सूखे पत्ते खाए, बाद में केवल जल पर जीवन बिताया और अंततः निराहार रहकर केवल शिव नाम के जप पर जीवित रहीं। इस कठोर तप के कारण इनका नाम "अपर्णा" भी पड़ा।

एक बार स्वयं भगवान शिव ने ब्राह्मण के वेश में आकर उनकी परीक्षा ली और शिव की निन्दा की। माँ ने क्रोध में उन्हें वहाँ से जाने को कहा - तब शिव जी ने अपना असली स्वरूप प्रकट किया और माँ की भक्ति को स्वीकार किया। इस प्रकार इनकी तपस्या सफल हुई।

माँ ब्रह्मचारिणी की उपासना से स्वाधिष्ठान चक्र जागृत होता है। इस दिन श्वेत या नारंगी वस्त्र धारण करें, शक्कर का भोग लगाएँ और ॐ ब्रह्मचारिण्यै नमः का जप करें। इनकी कृपा से तप, त्याग, वैराग्य, संयम और सत्य की प्राप्ति होती है।
