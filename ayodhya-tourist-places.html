Ayodhya's Sacred Pilgrimage Circuit

Every Place That Awaits the Devoted Traveller

Ayodhya is far more than a single temple — it is a living city woven with divine sites, sacred ghats, royal palaces, and heritage shrines spanning multiple traditions. Whether you have one day or several, this guide covers all 20 must-visit destinations, organised by their proximity to the Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir.

lightbulb

Pilgrim's Starting Point

All distances listed are measured approximately from Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Mandir as the central reference point. Most sites within 3 km are easily walkable or accessible by e-rickshaw. Sites at 7–9 km (Guptar Ghat, Surya Kund, Gulab Bari) are best reached by auto-rickshaw or cab.

✦ Core Sanctum Zone — 0 to 1 km ✦
01
Shri Ram Janmabhoomi Temple
location_on Starting point · Ramkot, Ayodhya
temple_hindu Religious / Temple Principal Attraction

The sacred birthplace of Lord Shri Ram and the principal pilgrimage attraction of Ayodhya. The grand Nagara-style temple complex — inaugurated in 2024 — stands at the very site of Ram Lalla's divine birth. Every pilgrimage to Ayodhya begins and centres here.

star Best for: Ram Lalla Darshan, Parikrama, Surya Tilak event (Ram Navami), Annakshetra Maha Prasad
02
Hanuman Garhi
location_on ~0.5–1 km · Ramkot, Ayodhya
temple_hindu Religious / Temple

One of Ayodhya's most revered Hanuman temples, approached by a grand staircase of 76 steps. Devotees traditionally seek Hanuman Ji's blessings here before proceeding for Ram darshan — a practice followed by pilgrims for centuries. The temple is believed to have served as a protective shrine for the city.

star Best for: Darshan, spirituality, photography, local culture
03
Kanak Bhawan
location_on ~0.5–0.8 km · Tulsi Nagar, Ayodhya
temple_hindu Religious / Temple

A beautiful temple dedicated to Lord Ram and Goddess Sita, celebrated for its ornate golden architecture and deeply devotional atmosphere. According to tradition, the palace was gifted to Mata Sita by Kaikeyi. The temple's golden spires and richly adorned deity images make it one of Ayodhya's most photographed shrines.

star Best for: Architecture, Sita-Ram darshan, devotional atmosphere, photography
04
Dashrath Mahal
location_on ~0.5–1 km · Ramkot, Ayodhya
temple_hindu Religious / Heritage

A popular religious site associated with King Dashrath, the father of Lord Ram. Its colourful entrance gates and devotional surroundings evoke the grandeur of the Ikshvaku royal heritage. An essential stop in the old-city temple circuit for anyone tracing Ayodhya's Ramayana connections.

star Best for: Ramayana heritage walk, darshan, old-city exploration
07
Sita Ki Rasoi
location_on ~0.5–1 km · Near Ram Janmabhoomi
temple_hindu Religious / Shrine

A traditional religious site associated with Mata Sita, located near the Janmabhoomi complex. The name refers to Sita's symbolic kitchen, and the site upholds the sacred tradition of serving food and charity (Annadaan) to devotees — a living act of bhakti in Ayodhya's social fabric.

star Best for: Sita bhakti, Annadaan seva, cultural tradition
✦ Sarayu & Heritage Belt — 1 to 3 km ✦
05
Ram Ki Paidi
location_on ~1.5 km · Banks of the Sarayu
water Ghat / Waterfront

A spectacular series of ghats along the sacred Sarayu River, especially magnificent during the evening when the entire riverfront and stone steps are illuminated in golden light. The Ayodhya administration identifies Ram Ki Paidi as one of the city's most important places of interest. The Deepotsav celebrations here are legendary.

star Best for: Evening Aarti, holy dip, Deepotsav, Sarayu Snaan, sunset views
06
Nageshwar Nath Temple
location_on ~1.5–2 km · Near Ram Ki Paidi
temple_hindu Shiva Temple

A historic Shiva temple situated near Ram Ki Paidi, traditionally associated with the ancient religious heritage of Ayodhya and linked to Kush, son of Lord Ram. An important stop for Shiva devotees exploring Ayodhya's broader pilgrimage circuit, often visited alongside the Sarayu ghats.

star Best for: Shiva darshan, Mahashivratri, Sarayu ghat walk
08
Treta Ke Thakur
location_on ~2 km · Near Naya Ghat
temple_hindu Religious / Temple

A revered temple traditionally associated with Lord Ram's Treta Yuga era, believed to mark the site of the Ashwamedha Yagna performed by Lord Ram. An important stop for pilgrims exploring Ayodhya's Ramayana-related sites, situated near the scenic Naya Ghat riverfront area.

star Best for: Ramayana heritage, pilgrimage circuit, devotional exploration
09
Deokaali Temple
location_on ~2–3 km · Ayodhya
temple_hindu Shakti Shrine

An important Shakti shrine deeply woven into Ayodhya's religious traditions, with devotional stories connected to Mata Sita. Listed by the Ayodhya district tourism authority among its notable religious attractions, the temple draws both Shakta pilgrims and Ram devotees exploring the wider sacred geography.

star Best for: Shakti darshan, Navratri celebration, diverse pilgrimage
10
Mani Parvat
location_on ~2–3 km · Kami Ganj, Ayodhya
landscape Sacred Hill

A small but spiritually significant sacred hill embedded in Ayodhya's Ramayana traditions. Climbing its gentle slopes offers a distinctive elevated perspective of the surrounding city and sacred landscape. Associated with the Sanjeevani herb episode, this site adds a unique dimension to the pilgrimage circuit.

star Best for: Panoramic views, nature walk, Ramayana heritage
11
Brahma Kund
location_on ~2–3 km · Ayodhya
water Sacred Kund

A historic sacred water site of ancient religious importance in Ayodhya's spiritual landscape. The nearby Brahma Kund Gurudwara further enriches this area, making it a fascinating stop for visitors exploring Ayodhya's remarkably diverse spiritual heritage across Hindu and Sikh traditions.

star Best for: Sacred bathing, multi-faith heritage, quiet reflection
14
Valmiki Bhawan
location_on ~2–3 km · Karsewakpuram
menu_book Literary / Religious

A religious site associated with Maharishi Valmiki, the revered sage traditionally credited as the composer of the Valmiki Ramayana — the Adi Kavya. A quieter, more contemplative addition to the main Ayodhya pilgrimage circuit, ideal for those seeking the literary and philosophical roots of the Ram Katha.

star Best for: Ramayana scholars, quiet darshan, literary pilgrimage
16
Tulsi Smarak Bhawan
location_on ~2–3 km · Ayodhya
history_edu Cultural / Literary

A cultural and literary attraction dedicated to Goswami Tulsidas and the living tradition of the Ramcharitmanas. This bhawan preserves manuscripts, illustrations, and exhibits celebrating the greatest Hindi devotional work. Especially meaningful for visitors interested in the literary and cultural legacy of the Ram Katha tradition.

star Best for: Ramcharitmanas tradition, cultural heritage, literary pilgrimage
15
Jain Shwetambar Temple
location_on ~3–4 km · Awas Vikas Colony
temple_hindu Jain Temple

Ayodhya holds deep significance in the Jain tradition as the birthplace of multiple Tirthankaras, including Rishabhadeva (Adinath). This Shwetambar temple represents the city's rich Jain heritage and is an invaluable stop for visitors interested in India's multifaceted religious history and the connections between ancient faiths.

star Best for: Jain heritage, interfaith exploration, history enthusiasts
19
Chhoti Chhawni
location_on ~3–4 km · Ayodhya
apartment Heritage Complex

A distinctive heritage complex known for its architectural character and religious significance. Chhoti Chhawni offers visitors another perspective on Ayodhya beyond the principal Ram temples — a glimpse into the layered history and built fabric of the older quarters of the city.

star Best for: Architecture, heritage walks, city history
20
Birla Temple
location_on ~4–5 km · Ayodhya–Faizabad Road
temple_hindu Religious / Temple

A relatively modern and well-maintained temple dedicated to Lord Ram and Goddess Sita, situated on the Ayodhya–Faizabad road. Listed among the city's tourist attractions by the Ayodhya district tourism portal, it provides a serene and accessible darshan experience en route to or from the city.

star Best for: Darshan, en-route visit, peaceful atmosphere
✦ Outlying Sacred Sites — 7 to 9 km ✦
13
Surya Kund
location_on ~7–8 km · Darshan Nagar
water Temple & Kund

A religious site dedicated to Surya Dev (the Sun God), housing a sacred kund and temple complex in the Darshan Nagar area. Associated with Ayodhya's solar heritage — the Ikshvaku dynasty being descendants of the Sun — this is an enriching stop for pilgrims wanting to explore meaningful attractions beyond central Ayodhya.

star Best for: Surya worship, Chhath Puja, Ikshvaku heritage
17
Gulab Bari
location_on ~7–8 km · Faizabad
park Heritage Garden

A historic garden-tomb complex associated with Nawab Shuja-ud-Daula of Awadh, famed for its rose gardens and Nawabi architectural elegance. It provides a tranquil change of scenery from Ayodhya's temples and offers an illuminating glimpse into the Nawabi heritage of the broader Ayodhya-Faizabad region. Listed among the district's places of interest.

star Best for: Nawabi history, garden walks, Awadh heritage
18
Moti Mahal
location_on ~7–8 km · Faizabad
apartment Nawabi Heritage

A heritage attraction connected with the Nawabi history of Faizabad, representing the architectural legacy of the Awadh rulers. Best combined with Gulab Bari for visitors interested in the historic architecture and cultural heritage of the Faizabad-Ayodhya region — a rewarding half-day excursion.

star Best for: Nawabi architecture, Faizabad history, heritage tourism
12
Guptar Ghat
location_on ~9 km · Faizabad Cantt / Sarayu
water Sacred Ghat

One of the most spiritually significant ghats beyond the central temple cluster, traditionally associated with Lord Ram's departure from the earthly realm (Jal Samadhi). Set along the peaceful Sarayu, it is especially serene and contemplative around sunset — a place of deep stillness and reverence for devoted pilgrims.

star Best for: Sunset darshan, meditation, boat ride, spiritual contemplation
