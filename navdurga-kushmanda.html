Maa Kushmanda
Creator of the Universe

The fourth of the nine divine forms of Nav Durga - and the most cosmically ancient. Maa Kushmanda created the entire universe from the void with a single divine smile. She dwells in the core of the sun itself, radiating light and life across all of creation.

01 - IDENTITY

Who is Maa Kushmanda?

"Ku" means little, "Ushma" means energy/warmth, "Anda" means cosmic egg. She is the one who created the universe - the little warm egg of creation - from nothing but her own divine smile and inner light.

wb_sunny
Lives in the Sun
She resides within the solar disc - the surya mandala - giving the sun its light and life-giving energy. She is the divine power behind the sun itself, the source of all warmth in the cosmos.
back_hand
Ashtabhuja - Eight Arms
Her eight arms hold kamandal, bow, arrow, lotus, amrit kalash, chakra, gada - and uniquely - a japa mala in the eighth hand, joining her divine weapons with her devotional practice.
pets
Vahana: Lion
She rides a lion - symbol of royal power, authority and divine courage. The lion carries the goddess who gave birth to all creation, symbolising that the universe itself rests on divine strength.
favorite
Chakra: Anahata
Her worship activates the Anahata chakra - the heart centre. As the one who created all life with love, she awakens the heart chakra, opening devotees to joy, compassion and creative energy.

सुरासम्पूर्णकलशं रुधिराप्लुतमेव च।
दधाना हस्तपद्माभ्यां कूष्माण्डा शुभदास्तु मे॥

Surāsampūrṇakalaśaṃ rudhirāplutameva ca, Dadhānā hastapadmābhyāṃ Kūṣmāṇḍā śubhadāstu me.

"She who holds in her lotus hands a pot filled with the nectar of the gods and one filled with blood - may Maa Kushmanda bestow auspiciousness upon me." This shloka encapsulates her role as sustainer of both creation and its cycles of life and dissolution.

- Devi Mahatmya · Navratri Dhyana Shloka

02 - THE CREATION STORY

The Smile That Lit the Universe

Before there was anything - no sun, no stars, no earth, no time - there was darkness and void. And within that void was the goddess.

dark_mode

Before Creation - Endless Void

In the beginning there was nothing - no light, no matter, no time. The scriptures describe this as the state before even Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva existed in their current forms. All that existed was the Adi Shakti - the primordial energy of the universe - waiting within the darkness.

sentiment_very_satisfied

The Divine Smile

The goddess smiled - a single, spontaneous expression of divine joy from within the infinite darkness. That smile radiated light. That light became the first energy. And from that energy, the brahmanda - the cosmic egg - was born. The universe had begun.

↓
wb_sunny

She Entered the Sun

After setting creation in motion, Maa Kushmanda took up residence within the solar disc - the most powerful source of energy in our solar system. She sustains the sun's radiance, and through the sun, sustains all life on earth. She is the life-force behind every sunrise.

↓
auto_awesome

The Most Ancient Form

Of all Nav Durga, Kushmanda is the most cosmically primordial. The other forms - Shailputri, Brahmacharini, Chandraghanta - are manifestations within creation. But Kushmanda preceded creation. She is Adi Shakti at her most original, most vast, most awe-inspiring.

Beej Mantra · Day 4

ॐ कूष्माण्डायै नमः

Om Kūṣmāṇḍāyai Namah

Chant 108 times on Day 4. Her worship dispels disease, increases vitality and fills the devotee with creative energy. Offer yellow flowers and kumhde ka prasad (pumpkin-based offering) for her special blessing.

03 - PUJA VIDHI

How to Worship Maa Kushmanda on Day 4

01
Wear Yellow or Orange

Yellow is the colour of the sun - Maa Kushmanda's abode. Wearing bright yellow or orange on Day 4 is said to attract her solar blessings of health, vitality and positive energy.

02
Offer Malpua as Bhog

The traditional bhog for Maa Kushmanda is malpua - sweet pancakes. Some traditions also offer pumpkin (kumhda) in her worship, as her name itself contains a reference to it. Offering with devotion is said to bring long life and good health.

03
Worship Facing the Rising Sun

On Day 4, begin her puja facing east - the direction of the rising sun. Since she lives in the solar disc, worshipping her as the sun rises is the most aligned and powerful time for her invocation.

04
Chant Her Mantra 108 Times

Use a rudraksha mala and chant ॐ कूष्माण्डायै नमः 108 times. This activates the Anahata (heart) chakra and is said to fill the devotee with creative vitality and freedom from illness.

05
Read Durga Saptashati Chapter 9

Day 4 aligns with chapter 9 of Devi Mahatmya - the goddess's final praise hymns. Meditating on Kushmanda's cosmic form while listening to these praises connects the devotee to the primordial source of all shakti.

04 - TEACHINGS

What Kushmanda Teaches Us

sentiment_very_satisfied

Joy is Creative Power

She created the universe not from effort or struggle, but from a smile - a spontaneous act of joy. The teaching: genuine joy and delight are themselves creative forces. When you act from the deepest happiness within you, you create something real.

wb_sunny

Be a Source, Not a Receiver

She lives in the sun and radiates outward endlessly. Her teaching invites us to become sources of light - of warmth, energy and sustenance - for those around us, rather than constantly seeking to receive.

hub

Everything Began with Shakti

Before any god, any law, any element - there was Shakti. Kushmanda teaches the primacy of divine feminine energy in all of creation. The universe is not maintained by force alone; it is born from and sustained by love.

माँ कूष्माण्डा - मुस्कान से ब्रह्माण्ड रचने वाली आदिशक्ति

नवरात्रि के चतुर्थ दिन माँ कूष्माण्डा की पूजा की जाती है। "कु" का अर्थ है छोटा, "ऊष्मा" का अर्थ है ऊर्जा और "अण्ड" का अर्थ है ब्रह्माण्ड - अर्थात् जिन्होंने अपनी मन्द हास्य से इस छोटे ब्रह्माण्ड की रचना की। ये अष्टभुजा देवी हैं और सिंह पर आरूढ़ हैं।

जब सृष्टि में चारों ओर अन्धकार था और कुछ भी अस्तित्व में नहीं था, तब माँ कूष्माण्डा ने अपनी मन्द मुस्कान से इस ब्रह्माण्ड की रचना की। ये सूर्यमण्डल के भीतर निवास करती हैं और सूर्य को उसकी ऊर्जा प्रदान करती हैं। इनकी भक्ति से समस्त रोग-शोक नष्ट होते हैं और आयु, यश, बल तथा आरोग्य की प्राप्ति होती है।

इस दिन पीले या नारंगी वस्त्र पहनें, मालपुआ का भोग लगाएँ और उगते सूर्य की दिशा में मुँह करके पूजा करें। ॐ कूष्माण्डायै नमः का 108 बार जप करने से अनाहत चक्र जागृत होता है और जीवन में आनन्द, सृजन-शक्ति और स्वास्थ्य की वृद्धि होती है।
