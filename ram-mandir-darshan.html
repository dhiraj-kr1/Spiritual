Temple Guidelines

Maintaining the Sanctity of Shri Ram Janmbhoomi

All devotees are requested to follow these guidelines sincerely to preserve the sanctity, security, and peaceful atmosphere of the sacred Mandir premises.

no_photography
No Mobile / Photography

Mobile phones, cameras, and electronic devices are strictly not permitted inside the sanctum. Deposit at free lockers at Pilgrim Facilitation Centre before entry.

checkroom
Dress Modestly

Traditional attire (dhoti-kurta, saree, salwar-kameez) is recommended. Western shorts and sleeveless tops are not permitted inside the Mandir premises.

do_not_step
Remove Footwear

Shoes and sandals must be deposited at the designated footwear counters outside the Mandir. Free token-based shoe keeping is available.

no_food
No Outside Food / Flowers

Outside food, sweets, flowers, and garlands are not permitted inside. Prasad and flowers offered at the Mandir are available at the trust-managed counters.

back_hand
Queue Discipline

Please follow queue barriers and CISF instructions. Queue-jumping or crowd disruption may result in removal from the premises. Senior citizens have a separate queue.

volume_off
Maintain Silence

Loud conversations, music from devices, or shouting are prohibited inside the Mandir. Maintain a peaceful, devotional atmosphere at all times inside the sanctum.

smoking_rooms
No Smoking / Tobacco / Alcohol

Smoking, chewing tobacco, pan, gutka, and consumption of alcohol are strictly prohibited within the entire Mandir complex and surrounding Janmbhoomi Path.

security
Security Check Mandatory

All pilgrims must pass through CISF security checks at the entry gate. Carry a valid photo ID. Prohibited items will be confiscated at the entry point.

accessible
Facilities for Differently-Abled

Wheelchair access, separate queues, and dedicated CISF assistance are available for senior citizens and differently-abled devotees. Request at the PFC counter.

How to Reach

Ram Mandir is Well-Connected by Air, Train & Road

flight
By Air

Maharishi Valmiki International Airport (AYJ) — 12 km from Mandir. Direct flights from Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad & Bengaluru.

train
By Train

Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY) — 1.5 km from Mandir. Daily Vande Bharat and Amrit Bharat trains from major cities.

directions_bus
By Road

130 km from Lucknow • 200 km from Varanasi • 636 km from Delhi via Purvanchal Expressway.

electric_rickshaw
EV Shuttle (Ram Path)

Free EV shuttles run every 5 minutes on Ram Path connecting the railway station and major ghats to Mandir gate.

directions_walk
Bhakti Path (On Foot)

750-metre pedestrianised lane with shaded canopies, wheelchair access, and chilled water points leading to the Mandir gate.

local_taxi
Auto / Cab

Auto-rickshaws and taxis are available near Ayodhya Dham Station. Ola and Rapido operate from Lucknow and Gorakhpur.

For complete travel details, distances, and local transport guide — visit our How to Reach Ayodhya page.

Prasad

The Sacred Blessing of Ram Lalla — Offered to All Devotees

volunteer_activism
Maha Prasad (Free)
Shri Ram Janmbhoomi Trust

The Shri Ram Janmbhoomi Teerth Kshetra Trust distributes Maha Prasad free of cost to all devotees at designated counters inside the Pilgrim Facilitation Centre after darshan. It consists of laddoo, panchamrit, and tulsi.

shopping_bag
Prasad Counter (Paid)
Authentic Temple Prasad

Trust-authorised prasad counters on Janmbhoomi Path offer beautifully packaged boondi laddoo, Ayodhya peda, and assorted prasad packets that devotees can carry home as a sacred memento. Only trust-authorized counters are legitimate.

local_shipping
Prasad by Post
Home Delivery (India Post)

Devotees unable to visit Ayodhya can receive Ram Lalla Prasad at their doorstep through the India Post Prasad Scheme. The prasad package includes a special consecrated laddoo, sindoor, and Janmbhoomi soil packet.

warning
Beware of Unauthorised Sellers
Important Security Notice

Only purchase prasad from official Shri Ram Janmbhoomi Trust counters inside the PFC and on Janmbhoomi Path. Roadside vendors claiming to sell temple prasad outside the premises are not authorised by the Trust.

info Chappan Bhog: On special occasions and festivals including Ram Navami, Janmashtami, and Deepotsav, a grand 56-item Chappan Bhog is offered to Ram Lalla. Prasad from this offering is distributed to all devotees present at the Mandir on that day, completely free of charge.
