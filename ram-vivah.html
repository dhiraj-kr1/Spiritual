✦ Divine Confluence ✦

Ram Vivah Mahotsav

Celebrated each year with unparalleled fervor across Ayodhya Dham and Janakpur, the Ram Vivah Mahotsav enshrines the sacred institution of familial duty, pure devotion, and righteous marriage.

In Vedic theology, the marriage of Shri Ram and Mata Sita is revered as a kaulachara sambandha — a bond forged across cosmic ages. The Valmiki Ramayana narrates that only Shri Ram, carrying the dignity of Raghuvansha and the blessings of Vishwamitra, could fulfil the divine condition set by Maharaj Janak.

Throughout Ayodhya and beyond, the Ram Vivah Mahotsav is a synthesis of Chhattis Vidhi (thirty-six ritual steps), melodious Vivah Geets reverberating from household to palace, and the Avadhi tradition's vivid expression of domestic bliss and regal splendor. Thousands of Ramlilas restage this event every Margashirsha, uniting communities in devotion.

Ram–Sita Vivah: Dhanush Pinaka Bhanjan

King Janak proclaimed that only the fearsome capable of lifting Shiva's Pinaka would win Janaki's hand. Across kingdoms and age, this powerful declaration drew the mightiest of kings, providing an unmatched test of dharmic eligibility.

The Graceful Step of Shri Ram

✦ The Royal Journey ✦

The Grand Ram Baraat

From the sun-kissed ghats of Saryu in Ayodhya to the fragrant mango groves of Janakpur, Maharaj Dashrath led the most magnificent Baraat in living memory, adorned with grand chariots, Vedic drums, and auspicious joy.

①

Ayodhya's Royal Preparation

Maharaj Dashrath's preparations began weeks prior. The city of Ayodhya was adorned with fragrant flowers, golden lamps, and colourful rangoli patterns in every lane and by-lane. Citizens offered prayers and songs of celebration along the entire royal path.

②

The Sacred Pilgrimage

Travelling through ancient pilgrimage routes, the processional halted to bathe at sacred tirthas where Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati confluences were revered with the entire entourage offering holy oblations before the night's rest.

③

Janakpur Sumilk Milap

Janakpur's pristine surroundings came alive as Maharaj Janak's envoys welcomed the Baraat at the city's boundary with flowers, Maithili folk songs, and conch shells in a grand ceremony that left all the princes deeply moved.

④

The Fourfold Nuptial

Besides Shri Ram and Mata Sita, Bharat was united with Mandavi, Lakshmana with Urmila, and Shatrughna with Shrutakirti — uniting all the holy princes of Raghuvansh with the daughters of the Janaka and Kushwadhwaja lineages.

✦ Vivah Matrimonial Rites ✦

Sacred Mandap Ceremony

Conducted under the auspicious Ram Vivah Mandap adorned with pearls, aromatic wood, and ancient Vedic fires presided over by the learned Shatananda and Vasishtha Maharishi.

Jaimala (Varmala)

The Sacred Garland Exchange

Mata Sita garlanded Bhagwan Ram amid divine showers of flowers from the heavens. The exchange of garlands symbolized the voluntary, free-willed acceptance of one another as eternal soulmates, fulfilling Shakti and Shiva's cosmic purpose.

Havan & Agni Pradakshina

The Sacred Circumambulation

Seven circumambulations of the sacred fire altar — each phera representing a vow: mutual companionship, sustenance, prosperity, health, devotion, a lasting bond, and eternal friendship through the cosmic cycles of creation.

Saptapadi (The Seven Vows)

The Eternal Vows of Grihastha

While traversing the fire's witness, each sacred vow bound Ram and Sita in an eternal spiritual covenant; dharma was thus enshrined as the very cornerstone of their divine marriage — a model for all Sanatana households through all ages.

Kanyadaan Ceremony

Janak's Supreme Gift

King Janak, moved to tears, formally gave Mata Sita's hand in Kanyadaan — the greatest of all gifts that a father can bestow upon his daughter — accompanied by the recitation of the Vedas and proclamations of eternal duty.

Sindoor Daan

The Sacred Vermilion Rite

Shri Ram applied the sacred vermilion into Mata Sita's hair partition, completing the most intimate and tender moment of the wedding — a rite that forever marks the wife's protected, beloved status in Sanatana tradition.

GoBhadhan & Ashirwad

Divine Blessings & Charity

The joyous occasion concluded with the distribution of alms to thousands of Brahmins, cows gifted in abundance, and all the sages and assembled devotees offering their heartfelt blessings of lifelong happiness, dharma, and devotion to the newlyweds.

✦ Dharma & Moral Philosophy ✦

Ram Katha — Eternal Principles of Vivah

Beyond the joy and festivity, the Ram Vivah encapsulates eternal Dharmic principles illuminating how the ideal human relationship flourishes from the bedrock of Shri Ram and Sita's life.

The Inviolable Vow of Grihastha Dharma

Unlike ordinary marriages, Shri Ram and Sita's wedding was a Swayamvara of dharma — the whole universe assented, devas rained flowers, and Rishis pronounced blessings with the authority of the Vedas. Their Grihastha Ashrama remains the ultimate embodiment of righteous household life marked by mutual respect, sacrifice, and ever-present devotion to duty. The Ramayana insists that a wife is not a possession but the very ardhaangini — the other half — of man's soul.

Maryada & Dharma

Maryada & Dharma

Ram's every action at the Vivah was bounded by Dharma. He neither rushed nor faltered but acted as the perfect son, student, and bridegroom — modeling for all time the highest conduct of a noble man entering household life.

Ananya Bhakti

Ananya Bhakti and Shraddha

Mata Sita's surrender was not of weakness but of supreme devotion — a chosen commitment, Ananya Bhakti — that she demonstrated by lifting the Shiva Dhanush as a child, thereby revealing her own divine equipoise and cosmic role.

Yaydri Harmony

Family Harmony and Vivaha Satva

The Ram Vivah forever enshrined harmony — not just between the couple but among all four brothers, both families, the assembled sages, devas, and even the natural world, which is the very standard of Vivaha Satva in Sanatan Dharma.

The Regal Story

The Regal Story on eternal Jivan

Understanding the Ram Vivah as a philosophical and social event, scholars across centuries have expounded how this sacred union elevated the status of women, sanctified the institution of marriage, and established devotion as the highest human quality.

✦ Bhaktiveshi Meeting Wellness ✦

Sacred Vivah Mahaprasad

On the auspicious occasion of Ram Vivah Mahotsav, the Mahaprasad is specially prepared with pure ingredients and distributed with deep generosity at temples across Ayodhya.

Primary Prasadam

Kanak Bhavan Makhan

Sweetened churned butter offered at Kanak Bhavan — Ram's beloved treat — distributed to all devotees as the most intimate prasad of the divine couple.

Sacred Sweets

Panchaamrit & Panjiri

A sacred mix of whole wheat flour roasted in pure ghee with dry fruits, offered to evoke the sweetness of the celestial Vivah and blessed by the presiding priests.

Auspicious Offering

Meethan Laddoo

Made from boondi or besan by devout Brahmins following ancient recipes preserved in the temple kitchens, offered during the Vivah Aarti and distributed to all attending devotees.

Divine Blessing

Janam Kirtana Bhandara

A massive community feast open to all — no distinction of caste, creed, or background — echoing Ramrajya's eternal principle that in Ram's kingdom all are equal, all are welcome, and all are beloved.
