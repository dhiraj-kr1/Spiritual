Maa Shailputri
Daughter of the Mountains

The first of the nine divine forms of Maa Durga worshipped during Navratri - Shailputri, the daughter of Himavan, the mountain king. She is Sati reborn, Parvati in her purest mountain form, the eternal shakti who sets the spiritual journey of all nine nights in motion.

01 - IDENTITY

Who is Maa Shailputri?

"Shaila" means mountain, "Putri" means daughter. She is the divine shakti who took birth as the daughter of Himavan - the personification of the Himalayas - and is worshipped as the very first form of Nav Durga.

landscape
Two Hands
She holds a trishul (trident) in her right hand - the weapon of Shiva - and a lotus flower in her left, symbolising purity and grace together.
pets
Vahana: Nandi
She rides Nandi, the sacred bull who is also Lord Shiva's vehicle - an early symbol of her eternal connection to Mahadeva, even before their divine marriage.
brightness_7
Chakra: Muladhara
Her worship is said to activate the Muladhara chakra - the root chakra at the base of the spine - the foundation of all spiritual energy and earthly stability.
palette
Colour: Royal Blue
Devotees wear royal blue or grey on Day 1 of Navratri. These colours represent strength, stability, and the vast sky above the mountains she was born from.

वन्दे वाञ्छितलाभाय चन्द्रार्धकृतशेखराम्।
वृषारूढां शूलधरां शैलपुत्रीं यशस्विनीम्॥

Vande vāñchita-lābhāya candrārdha-kṛta-śekharām, Vṛṣārūḍhāṃ śūladharāṃ Śailaputrīṃ yaśasvinīm.

"I bow to Maa Shailputri - she who grants all desired boons, who bears a crescent moon on her crown, who rides a bull, who holds the trident, and who is forever glorious." This is the dhyana shloka chanted at the beginning of her worship on Navratri Day 1.

- Devi Mahatmya · Navratri Dhyana Shloka

02 - STORY

The Three Births of the Goddess

To understand Shailputri is to follow the Devi through her previous divine births - each a chapter in the eternal love between Shakti and Shiva.

I

Sati - Daughter of Daksha

In her first birth, the goddess was born as Sati, daughter of Prajapati Daksha. She fell in love with Shiva and married him against her father's wishes. When Daksha organised a grand yagna and deliberately insulted Shiva by not inviting him, Sati attended - and unable to bear her husband's humiliation, she immolated herself in the sacred fire. This act of ultimate devotion and protest shook the cosmos itself.

First Birth · As Daksha's Daughter
II

Shailputri - Daughter of Himavan

In her second birth, the same divine Shakti was reborn as Shailputri - daughter of Himavan, the king of the Himalayas, and his queen Mena. Born amidst the eternal snows and sacred peaks, she retained the memory of her previous devotion to Shiva. This is the form we worship on Navratri Day 1 - the goddess in her mountain-born purity, before her great tapasya begins.

Second Birth · As Himavan's Daughter ✦ This Form
III

Parvati - The Perfect Half of Shiva

As Shailputri grew, she remembered her vow to be Shiva's wife. She performed intense tapasya - becoming Brahmacharini (Navratri Day 2) - and won Shiva's acceptance. After their divine marriage, she became Parvati, the beloved consort of Mahadeva, and eventually took on her fierce warrior forms as Durga and Kali. The Nav Durga series traces this entire journey across nine forms.

Third Expression · As Parvati, then Nav Durga
03 - PUJA VIDHI

How to Worship Maa Shailputri on Day 1

Navratri Day 1 begins with Ghatasthapana - the ceremonial installation of the kalash - followed by the worship of Maa Shailputri.

01
Ghatasthapana - Invoke the Kalash

Fill a copper or earthen pot with holy water, place mango leaves and a coconut on it, and install it on a bed of soil with sown seeds. This kalash represents the goddess herself and the fertile energy of the nine days ahead. It must be placed in the auspicious muhurta on the first morning.

02
Wear Royal Blue or Grey

Each day of Navratri has a dedicated colour for the goddess and for devotees. On Day 1, wear royal blue or grey - colours that honour Maa Shailputri's connection to the mountains, sky, and the strength she embodies.

03
Offer White Flowers & Cow's Milk

Maa Shailputri is especially pleased by white flowers - jasmine, white champa or white lotus. Her bhog (offering) on Day 1 traditionally includes pure cow's ghee and milk. Offering ghee on Day 1 is said to bless the devotee with good health and freedom from disease.

04
Chant Her Beej Mantra 108 Times

Using a rudraksha mala, chant her beej mantra ॐ ऐं ह्रीं क्लीं चामुण्डायै विच्चे or simply ॐ शैलपुत्र्यै नमः 108 times. This activates the Muladhara chakra and grounds the spiritual energy for the entire Navratri.

05
Read or Listen to Durga Saptashati (Chapter 1)

Each day of Navratri corresponds to a section of the Durga Saptashati (700 verses of Devi Mahatmya from the Markandeya Purana). Day 1 aligns with the first chapter - the story of Madhu and Kaitabha. Listening to or reading this with devotion is considered highly meritorious.

Beej Mantra · Day 1

ॐ शैलपुत्र्यै नमः

Om Shailputryai Namah

Chant this mantra 108 times with a pure heart and rudraksha mala on the first morning of Navratri. Offer a white flower with each round.

04 - SIGNIFICANCE

Why Shailputri is Worshipped First

The order of the Nav Durga forms is not accidental - it is a complete spiritual journey. Shailputri comes first for profound reasons.

foundation

Foundation of All Nine

The Muladhara chakra is the root - without its activation, the other eight chakras cannot be energised. Similarly, worship of Shailputri grounds the devotee before the ascent through the other eight forms.

favorite

The Original Devotion

Shailputri represents the goddess before her trials - innocent, pure, full of potential. She is the beginning of the Shakti story. To begin Navratri with her is to begin at the source.

self_improvement

Shakti in Her Purest Form

She has not yet fought demons, not yet performed tapasya, not yet become fierce. She is the complete, serene, mountain-born goddess - all power at rest. This is the most sattvic form of the Devi.

माँ शैलपुत्री - पर्वतराज हिमवान की दिव्य पुत्री

नवरात्रि के प्रथम दिन माँ शैलपुत्री की उपासना की जाती है। "शैल" का अर्थ है पर्वत और "पुत्री" का अर्थ है कन्या - अर्थात् पर्वतराज हिमवान की पुत्री। इनका वाहन वृषभ (नंदी) है और ये दाहिने हाथ में त्रिशूल तथा बाएँ हाथ में कमल पुष्प धारण करती हैं।

पूर्वजन्म में ये दक्ष प्रजापति की पुत्री सती थीं। अपने पिता के यज्ञ में भगवान शिव का अपमान न सह सकने के कारण उन्होंने यज्ञाग्नि में स्वयं को समर्पित कर दिया। पुनः हिमवान के यहाँ पुत्री के रूप में जन्म लेकर वे शैलपुत्री कहलाईं। आगे चलकर उन्होंने घोर तपस्या करके भगवान शिव को पतिरूप में प्राप्त किया और माँ पार्वती बनीं।

नवरात्रि के पहले दिन घटस्थापना की जाती है और माँ शैलपुत्री की पूजा आरम्भ होती है। इस दिन नीले या स्लेटी रंग के वस्त्र धारण करना शुभ माना जाता है। श्वेत पुष्प, गाय का दूध और घी अर्पित करने से देवी प्रसन्न होती हैं। इनकी उपासना से मूलाधार चक्र जागृत होता है और साधक की आध्यात्मिक यात्रा का शुभारम्भ होता है।

माँ शैलपुत्री समस्त शक्तियों का मूल स्रोत हैं। जैसे हिमालय की नींव पर ही समस्त नदियाँ, वन और जीवन टिका है, वैसे ही माँ शैलपुत्री की उपासना पर नवरात्रि की समग्र साधना टिकी है। उनका आशीर्वाद प्राप्त कर नवरात्रि का पुण्य आरम्भ होता है।
