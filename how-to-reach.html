Reaching Ayodhya Dham

Seamless Connectivity Across Air, Rail & Road

flight
By Air

The nearest airports to Ayodhya are:

  • check_circle Maharishi Valmiki International Airport, Ayodhyadham — 12 km from Ram Mandir. Direct flights to Delhi, Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru and Chennai.
  • check_circle Gorakhpur Airport (GOP) — 118 km from Ayodhya. Connected to Delhi, Mumbai and Kolkata.
  • check_circle Amausi Airport (LKO), Lucknow — 125 km from Ayodhya. International airport with widest flight connectivity.
train
By Train

Regular trains connect Ayodhya to other major cities across the country. Key railway stations:

  • check_circle Ayodhya Dham Junction (AY) — 1.5 km from Ram Mandir. Daily Vande Bharat, Amrit Bharat and superfast express trains from Delhi, Mumbai, Patna and Varanasi.
  • check_circle Ayodhya Cantt (AYC) — Secondary station serving additional routes from Gorakhpur and Faizabad division.
directions_bus
By Road

UPSRTC buses as well as private buses run at regular intervals from different cities. Approximate road distances:

Local Transport in Ayodhya

Getting Around Within the Holy City

Once you arrive in Ayodhya, the city is well-served by pilgrimage-friendly transport options connecting you to Ram Mandir and all major sacred sites.

electric_rickshaw
EV Shuttles (Ram Path)

Government EV shuttles run every 5 minutes on Ram Path, the main 13 km arterial connecting major ghats and the Mandir gate.

local_taxi
Auto-Rickshaws

Widely available near the railway station and bus stand. Pre-fixed rates for Mandir, Hanuman Garhi and Sarayu ghats.

directions_bus
City Buses (UPSRTC)

UPSRTC mini-buses connect Ayodhya railway station to Ram Mandir and nearby pilgrimage circuits at nominal fares.

car_rental
Cab / Taxi Services

Ola, Rapido and local cabs are available from Lucknow, Gorakhpur and the airport. Recommended for families with luggage.

two_wheeler
E-Rickshaws

E-rickshaws ply through Bhakti Path and residential lanes near the Mandir, ideal for short distances at affordable rates.

directions_walk
Bhakti Path (On Foot)

The 750-metre pedestrianised Bhakti Path from Ram Path to Mandir gate features shaded canopies and wheelchair lanes for a serene walk.

Helpful Travel Tips

A few thoughtful pointers to ensure your journey to Shri Ram Janmbhoomi is smooth, peaceful, and spiritually fulfilling.

01

Arrive Early: Start your journey so you reach Ayodhya before 7 AM. Early morning darshan queues are shorter and the atmosphere is deeply serene.

02

Book Trains in Advance: Vande Bharat trains to Ayodhya Dham are heavily booked during Ram Navami, Diwali, and weekends. Reserve tickets at least 2–3 weeks ahead.

03

Deposit Mobiles at PFC: Mobile phones are not permitted inside the Mandir sanctum. Use free locker facilities at the Pilgrim Facilitation Centre on Janmbhoomi Path.

04

Dress Modestly: Traditional attire is recommended. Dhoti-kurta, saree or salwar-kameez are appropriate for darshan. Shoes must be deposited at designated counters.

05

Stay Hydrated: Free chilled RO water points are available along Bhakti Path and inside PFC premises. Carry a small water bottle for the journey from the station.

06

Festival Season Caution: During Ram Navami and Deepotsav, Ayodhya receives lakhs of devotees daily. Plan an overnight stay in advance and arrive via early morning trains.
