✦ Spiritual Lineage & Heritage ✦

Reawakening the Golden Glory of Treta Yuga

When Maryada Purushottam Bhagwan Shri Ram, along with Janaki Mata and Hanuman Lakshmana, descended into the holy soil of Ayodhya after 14 years of forest exile and the historic victory over Ravana, the entire city erupted into an ecstatic tapestry of light. Not a single corner remained untouched by the resplendent glow of pure earthen lamps.

In Ayodhya Dham, Deepotsav stands as an experiential revival of that very spiritual memory. Celebrated annually on the eve of Diwali (Kartik Amavasya), it is not merely a civic spectacle, but a massive collective spiritual sadhana — a devotion of lighting millions of diyas to honour the sovereign spirit of peace, harmony, and righteous governance.

"The darkness of millennia melted away as if every household lit its lamp of gratitude, celebrating the arrival of Shri Rama upon the earth."

In modern Bharat, the Ayodhya Deepotsav stands as an experiential revival of that very spiritual testimony. Celebrated annually on the eve of Diwali (Kartik Amavasya), it is not merely once a spectacle, but a massive collective spiritual sadhana — a devotion of offering millions of earthen lamps to honour the sovereign spirit of peace, harmony, and righteous governance.

✦ Sarayu • Sapta Pranam ✦

Sarayu Ghat: The River of Liquid Gold

Witness the hallowed river where eons of devotion reflect in millions of floating flames, while Vedic priests invite divine grace across all three sacred worlds.

①
Deep Daan on the Sacred Waters

Devotees descending from marigold-decorated boats carrying glowing earthen lamps upon the current, creating an offering at Saryu's surface — thousands of tiny suns flowing on the sacred river's face.

②
The Grand 1,100 Priest Maha Aarti

Arranged symmetrically along the elevated stone steps of Rajghat, 1,100 consecrated priests simultaneously wave enormous flame-torches — displaying the night mantras, singing the heavenly hymns of Saryu to lakhs of devotees.

③
Guptar & Lakshman Ghat Visas

Extending for miles, continuous illuminated embellishments and artistic platforms of the riverside Ram Lakshmana create a focused and saintly descent, alighting it performs the tapasya.

✦ Sthanapal Epicenter ✦

Ram Ki Paidi: The Radiant Epicenter

An architecture of sacred water basins and cascading stone steps, converted into a living canvas of sacred geometric motifs and interlocking sacred eight-rayed chakras.

✦ Architectural Mapping

The Ramayana Chronicle on Ancient Temple Facade

Cutting-edge 3D projection systems transform the monumental classical structure, launching the Ramayana from Shri Rama's birth in Ayodhya — the exile of Ramji, Ram-Sita-Lakshmana's 14 years of forest, the coronation of Maa Keki, Ram's Lakshmana, Ram-Laxman into Lanka, Ram's victory and the crowning Rajyabhishek.

The Ramayana Chronide on Ancient Temple Facade draws the viewer across all seven Kand of the epic, as a river of imagery and light flows from the ramparts of the ancient ghats, offering a visual storytelling that transcends language and culture, speaking directly to the devotional heart of every assembled pilgrim.
