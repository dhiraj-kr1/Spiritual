108 Names of Maa Durga

The Durga Ashtottara Shatanamavali - 108 sacred names of the goddess, each a different facet of her infinite divine nature. Chanting these names daily, or during Navratri, is one of the most powerful forms of Devi upasana.

विधि - HOW TO CHANT

How to Practise the Namavali

wb_twilight
Best Time

Early morning (Brahma muhurta) or at dusk during Navratri. Sit facing east or north. Light a diya and offer flowers before beginning.

spa
With a Mala

Use a rudraksha or crystal mala of 108 beads. Chant each name as you pass one bead - completing one full round of the namavali.

local_florist
With Flowers

For deeper practice: offer one flower or petal at the feet of Devi's image with each name. This is the most meritorious form of the namavali puja.

favorite
The Fruit

Daily chanting of all 108 names is said to destroy all sins, remove fear and illness, and fill the devotee's life with the goddess's grace and protection.

या देवी सर्वभूतेषु शक्तिरूपेण संस्थिता।
नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमस्तस्यै नमो नमः॥

Yā devī sarvabhūteṣu śaktirūpeṇa saṃsthitā, Namastasyai namastasyai namastasyai namo namaḥ.

"To the goddess who dwells in all beings in the form of Shakti - salutation, salutation, salutation to her again and again." Begin and end the namavali with this verse from the Devi Mahatmya.

शरण्ये त्र्यम्बके गौरि नारायणि नमोऽस्तुते॥

Sarvamaṅgala-māṅgalye śive sarvārtha-sādhike, Śaraṇye tryambake gauri Nārāyaṇi namo'stute.

"O auspicious one, the most auspicious of all auspicious things - O Shiva, the fulfiller of all wishes - O refuge of all, O three-eyed one, O Gauri, O Narayani - salutation to you." End the namavali with this verse and offer flowers at Devi's feet.
