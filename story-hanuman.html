Hanuman defies the worldly contradiction between power and ego. While possessing the might to uproot mountains, his greatest pride remains sitting humbly at the lotus feet of Shri Ram.

03 — THE EPIC CHRONOLOGY

Five Epochs of Hanuman's Divine Odyssey

From his celestial birth as the incarnation of Shiva to his eternal vigil at Ayodhya's Hanumangarhi, explore the pivotal milestones of his journey.

1

Divine Incarnation & Discipleship with Surya Dev

Born to Mata Anjana and Kesari through the cosmic grace of Vayu and Shiva. As a divine child, he pursued Surya Dev across the skies; recognizing his dedication, the Sun God became his Guru, imparting the knowledge of the 4 Vedas and 6 Vedangas while moving across the firmament.

2

The Sacred Meeting with Shri Ram at Rishyamukha

Disguised as a young Vedic scholar, Hanuman approached Shri Ram and Lakshmana on Mount Rishyamukha. His sublime Sanskrit speech, flawless diction, and spiritual depth immediately captivated Shri Ram, who declared him a master of the Rig, Yajur, and Sama Vedas.

3

The Oceanic Leap & The Embassy to Lanka (Sundara Kanda)

Reminded of his latent cosmic powers by Jambavan on the shores of Mahendra Mountain, Hanuman expanded into Mahima form and leaped across the sea. He entered Ashoka Vatika, delivered the sacred Mudrika (ring) to Mother Sita, destroyed the grove of arrogance, and set Lanka ablaze with his ignited tail.

4

The Deliverer of Sanjeevani & Savior of the Vanara Sena

When Lakshmana fell unconscious on the battlefield of Lanka, Hanuman flew to the Himalayas. Unable to identify the luminescent herbs, he lifted the entire Dronagiri mountain and returned in hours, reviving Lakshmana and vanquishing the dread of adharma.

5

Consecration as Eternal Protector of Ayodhya Dham

Upon Shri Ram's coronation, Hanuman was gifted the highest citadel at Hanumangarhi. Before departing this earthly realm, Shri Ram consecrated Hanuman as the perpetual Kotwal (Divine Governor) of Ayodhya, declaring that pilgrims must seek Hanuman's blessings before entering the Janmbhoomi sanctum.
