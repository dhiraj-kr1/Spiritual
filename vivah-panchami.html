About Vivah Panchami

Vivah Panchami commemorates the sacred wedding anniversary of Bhagwan Shri Ram and Mata Sita, celebrating the divine union of Spiritual Consciousness (Purusha) and Material Existence (Cosmic Energy).

According to the ancient Hindu calendar, Vivah Panchami is celebrated on the fifth day (Panchami) of the bright half (Shukla Paksha) of the Margashirsha month. The ceremony marks the moment when Ram proved his worthiness to become a husband of Mata Sita by lifting and stringing Shiva's divine bow, the Pinaka — the bow of the divine self — and uniting the sacred dharma of kingship with the grace of goddess Shakti.

In the theological vision of the Ramcharitmanas, the stories of Shri Ram and Sita were established from their transcendent love and the deepest principles of Dharmic union emphasising respect, equality and holistic devotion that made each person greater through their sacred partnership. Their relationship is a model of conscious, sacred marriage — the union of Dharma and Karuna, strength and surrender, action and devotion.

✦ The Celestial Challenge ✦

Ram–Sita Vivah: The Swayamvar

King Janak proclaimed that only the man capable of lifting and stringing Lord Shiva's colossal bow, Pinaka, would win the hand of his beloved daughter Sita.

Framed by the Awakening

The Challenge of King Janak

His kingly wisdom and mighty kings gathered at this assembly. The Pinaka had rested who qualified on hundreds of holy men but none capable of lifting it. Sri Ram, led by Vishwamitra's wisdom through the many kings assembled, stood with dignity and readiness that captured every heart and proved his divine birthright.

जनक सुता जग जननि जानकी।
अतिसय प्रिय करुनानिधान की॥
Framed by a Holy Consecration

Vishwamitra's Command & Grace

Having giving Janak dependent yoga Vishwamitra proclaimed to the assembly and the devotion of Bhagwan Ram to the bow — "That 'O' Shiva Dhanush'", being known for all the world as a supreme manifestation of willpower, self-discipline and immense spiritual preparedness that the entire cosmos applauded.

लागु सरासन कर सरोज सुहाए।
हरहिं जनि रघुबीर मनहिं मन भाए॥
Framed by the Cosmic

The Shattering of Pinaka

From the very moment Shri Ram placed his fingers on the mighty Shiva Dhanush, an eagle moment of stillness pervaded all of creation. Then the shattering — a celestial thunder that resounded across the cosmos. From that sacred moment, the divine couple's destiny was sealed, witnessed by all the assembled sages and kings.

भंजेउ मुनि तव आयसु पाई।
सगुन सुमंगल बिजय बधाई॥
✦ The Royal Journey ✦

The Grand Ram Baraat

From the sun-kissed ghats of Saryu in Ayodhya to the fragrant mango groves of Janakpur, Maharaj Dashrath led the most magnificent Baraat in living memory, adorned with grand chariots, Vedic drums, and auspicious joy.

①

Ayodhya's Royal Preparation

Maharaj Dashrath's preparations began weeks prior. The city of Ayodhya was adorned with fragrant flowers, golden lamps, and colourful rangolis in every lane. Citizens offered prayers and joyful songs of celebration along the entire royal path as the magnificent Baraat was assembled.

②

The Sacred Pilgrimage Route

Travelling through ancient pilgrimage routes, the processional halted at sacred tirthas where Ganga, Yamuna, and Saraswati confluences were revered, with the entire entourage offering holy oblations at each stop before resuming the blessed journey to Mithila.

③

The Janakpur Welcoming

Janakpur's pristine surroundings came alive as Maharaj Janak's envoys welcomed the Baraat at the city boundary with flowers, Maithili folk songs, and conch shells in a grand ceremony that left all the princes deeply moved and the kingdoms united in joy.

④

The Fourfold Nuptial

Besides Shri Ram and Mata Sita, Bharat was united with Mandavi, Lakshman with Urmila, and Shatrughna with Shrutakirti — uniting all four holy princes of Raghuvansh with the daughters of the Janak and Kushwadhwaja lineages in one magnificent ceremony.

✦ Awadhi & Maithili Melodies ✦

Soulful Vivah Geet

Communities still keep alive the living tradition of Vivah Geets that generations of Awadhi and Maithili women have passed down, connecting the affective emotions of parenthood, kinship, and the sanctity of the ceremony.

Awadhi Tradition
Mithila's Gentle Teasing (Gali Geet)
ओ मेरे राजा लोक में मेरे राम के बरात आई।
ऐ भइया कुल सकल रघुबंश जोतिस आई॥

A playful women's song reflecting the exuberant joy of Janakpur's wives as they gleefully teased the handsome Ayodhya princes with verses of gentle mock — a beloved Maithili tradition that delights in poking fun at the groom's side.

Banna & Aarti
Awadhi Mangal Geet (Banna & Sehre)
सुन्दर सेहरा बाँधो रे रघुराज।
बाजे बधाई ढोल ताशे साज॥

Sung playfully by the women of Ayodhya's royal court during the elaborate pre-wedding rites, the Banna Geet praises the adorned bridegroom Sri Ram with comparisons to the brilliance of Lord Surya himself, greeting each milestone of the ceremony with festive refrains.

Kanya side
Kanyalaan & Sindoor Geet
सिय बिदाई के गीत विदाई।
मिथिले जनक की लाड़ली सीता विदाई॥

Vidai Geet — the most poignant of all wedding songs — sung as Mata Sita bids farewell to Janakpur. Tears flowed from the eyes of even the assembled sages and kings at this heart-stirring moment of separation of daughter from her father's kingdom and childhood home.

Raga & Laya
Sundarkan (The Sacred Farewell)
बधाई हो श्री जानकी रानी आई।
अयोध्यापुरी भई पुनीत, राम संग सीता आई॥

Sung as Mata Sita arrived at Ayodhya, this celebratory Badhai Geet filled the heavens with divine music as all of Ayodhya rushed out to receive the most extraordinary daughter-in-law ever to grace the Raghuvansha's sacred palace courts.

✦ Vivah Matrimonial Rites ✦

The Sacred Wedding Ceremony

Conducted under the holy canopy (Vivah Mandap) consecrated by Maharshi Vasishtha, presided over by the learned Shatananda and Maharishi Vishwamitra, and witnessed by all creation.

Jaimala (Varmala)

The Sacred Garland Exchange

Mata Sita garlanded Bhagwan Ram amid divine showers of flowers from the heavens. The exchange of Varmalas symbolized voluntary, free-willed acceptance of one another as eternal soulmates — seen and blessed by all the assembled gods, sages, and kings of creation.

Havan & Agni Pradakshina

The Sacred Circumambulation

Seven holy circumambulations of the sacred fire altar — each phera representing an eternal vow: mutual companionship, sustenance, prosperity, health, lifelong devotion, a lasting covenant bond, and eternal friendship through all cosmic cycles of creation and dissolution.

Saptapadi (The Seven Vows)

The Eternal Vows of Grihastha

While traversing with fire as witness, each sacred vow bound Ram and Sita in an eternal spiritual covenant. Dharma was thus enshrined as the very cornerstone of their divine marriage — an immortal model for all Sanatana households through every age of the universe.

Kanyadaan Ceremony

Janak's Supreme Offering

King Janak, moved to tears, formally gave Mata Sita's hand in the Kanyadaan — the greatest of all gifts a father can bestow — accompanied by the recitation of the Vedas, proclamations of eternal dharmic duty, and blessings of all the assembled Rishis.

Sindoor Daan

The Sacred Vermilion Rite

Shri Ram applied the sacred sindoor into Mata Sita's hair partition completing the most intimate and tender moment of the entire wedding — a rite that forever marks the wife's protected, beloved status as Ardhangini in the eternal Sanatana tradition.

GoBhadhan & Ashirwad

Divine Blessings & Charity

The joyous occasion concluded with the distribution of alms to thousands of Brahmins, cows gifted in abundance, and all the sages and assembled kings offering heartfelt blessings of lifelong happiness, dharmic righteousness, and eternal devotion to the blessed couple.

✦ Shobha Yatra in the Dham ✦

Grand Shobha Yatra in Ayodhya

On Vivah Panchami day, the city of Ayodhya transforms into a living festival beloved where devotees stream through the sacred streets re-enacting the ancient procession and offering their most sincere prayers.

The Royal Wedding Procession Across Ayodhya Dham

Organised under the patronage of the Shri Ram Janmbhoomi Trust, thousands of pilgrims, saints, families, and cultural troupes walk together for the traditional Yaatrapath — a re-enactment of the ancient Ram Baraat. Devotional tableaux depicting different scenes from the Vivah are carried on decorated palanquins, accompanied by Vedic hymns, folk music, and the fragrant smoke of sacred incense rising through the holy atmosphere.

The procession holds profound spiritual power for participants, as simply joining this sacred yatra is said to bestow the merit equivalent to attending the original divine wedding itself — an extraordinary blessing gifted to all by Bhagwan Ram's infinite compassion.

✦ Dharma & Moral Philosophy ✦

Ram Katha: Eternal Principles

Beyond the joy and festivity, Vivah Panchami illuminates eternal Dharmic principles that Shri Ram and Sita's life and sacred union will always demonstrate within the sacred tapestry of Shri Ram's Era.

balance
Maryada & Dharma

Maryada & Dharma

Ram's every action at the Vivah was bounded by Dharma. He neither rushed nor faltered but acted as the perfect son, student, and bridegroom — modelling for all time the highest conduct of a noble man entering sacred household life.

favorite
Ananya Bhakti

Ananya Bhakti and Shraddha

Mata Sita's surrender was not of weakness but of supreme devotion — an Ananya Bhakti — a chosen commitment she demonstrated by lifting the Shiva Dhanush as a child, revealing her own cosmic role as Shakti herself incarnate.

diversity_3
Family Harmony

Family Harmony and Vivaha Satva

The Ram Vivah forever enshrined harmony — not just between the couple but among all four brothers, both families, the assembled sages, devas, and even the natural world — a standard of Vivaha Satva in Sanatan Dharma for all ages.

brightness_5
Eka-Patni Vrata

Eka-Patni Vrata

Bhagwan Ram's lifelong commitment to Eka-Patni Dharma — fidelity to one wife across all trials — established the highest ideal of conjugal devotion, demonstrating that dharmic love does not waver in hardship or prosperity.

✦ Prasada & Puja Vitran ✦

The Sacred Vivah Mahaprasad

On the auspicious occasion of Vivah Panchami, the Mahaprasad is specially prepared with pure ingredients and distributed with deep generosity and love to all visiting devotees at temples across Ayodhya.

The Vivah Panchami Mahaprasad tradition holds that by partaking in the blessed food offerings on this holy day, devotees receive the spiritual merit of attending the original sacred wedding of Bhagwan Shri Ram and Mata Sita — an extraordinary blessing of grace and divine favour extended to all without distinction.

Primary Prasadam

Kanak Bhavan Makhan Mishri

Sweetened churned butter mixed with mishri crystals — Lord Ram's favourite childhood treat — prepared fresh at Kanak Bhawan and distributed to all devotees as the most intimate prasad of the divine couple on their wedding anniversary.

Sacred Sweets

Panchaamrit & Dhantiya Panjiri

A sacred mix of whole wheat flour roasted in pure ghee with crushed dry fruits and spices, this auspicious panjiri is offered to the deities first and then lovingly distributed to evoke the nuptial sweetness of the celestial Vivah ceremony.

Auspicious Offering

Tirupati Laddoo & Meethan

Made from gram flour by devout Brahmins following ancient temple kitchen recipes, these sacred laddoos are offered during the Vivah Panchami Aarti and distributed in large quantities to all devotees visiting the mandirs of Ayodhya on this blessed day.
