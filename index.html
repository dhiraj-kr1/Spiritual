place 01 — THE SACRED BIRTHPLACE

Where Faith Begins

Ayodhya, the sacred city of Shri Ram, has been revered for millennia as one of India's most spiritual centres. At its heart lies Shri Ram Janmabhoomi, regarded by devotees as the sacred birthplace of Shri Ram.

SCRIPTURAL LEGACY

Ashtachakra Navadvara

Described in ancient scriptures such as the Atharva Veda and Skanda Purana as a city of divine geometry — 'Ashtachakra Navadvara' — Ayodhya is celebrated as a divine abode mirroring celestial perfection and spiritual liberation.

Established by the Suryavamsha dynasty, the city flourished eternally along the serene and purifying waters of the Saryu river.

shield शाश्वत मूल्य • Eternal Values of Shri Ram

Ideals That Transcend Time

The life of Shri Ram continues to inspire generations through values that transcend time and place. These ideals form the spiritual foundation of his legacy and remain deeply rooted in the cultural consciousness of India.

balance
धर्म

Dharma

Dharma means the commitment to duty, righteousness, and living what is right. Shri Ram's journey exemplifies the ideal of placing dharma above personal desire and remaining steadfast in one's responsibilities.

verified
मर्यादा

Maryada

Maryada signifies honour, discipline, dignity, and the boundaries that guide righteous conduct. Shri Ram reminds us that Maryada is most meaningful when guided by empathy, humility, and compassion.

self_improvement
त्याग

Tyaga

Tyaga is the willingness to give up personal comfort or desire for a higher purpose. Shri Ram's journey exemplifies the strength of sacrifice, acceptance, and putting duty and the greater good before oneself.

handshake
करुणा

Karuna

Karuna is compassion — the ability to see and understand others' pain and respond with warmth and care. Shri Ram embodies karuna as a trait that enables the most meaningful relationships and actions when guided by empathy.
