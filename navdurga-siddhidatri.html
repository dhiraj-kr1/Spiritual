Maa Siddhidatri
Granter of All Siddhis

The ninth and final form of Nav Durga - Siddhidatri, the giver of all divine perfections. She sits serenely on a lotus, four-armed and radiant, bestowing the eight great siddhis upon her devotees and ultimately granting moksha - liberation itself. Even Lord Shiva worshipped her to obtain his divine powers. She is the culmination of the entire Navratri journey.

01 - IDENTITY

Who is Maa Siddhidatri?

"Siddhi" means divine perfection or supernatural attainment. "Datri" means the one who gives. She is the goddess who grants all eight great siddhis - and beyond them, moksha itself - to those who approach her with complete surrender.

local_florist
Seated on a Lotus
Unlike most Nav Durga who ride animals, Siddhidatri is seated on a fully bloomed lotus - the symbol of complete spiritual awakening, purity rising from the world, and the culmination of all spiritual endeavour.
back_hand
Four Arms
Her four hands hold a lotus, gada (mace), chakra and shankha (conch). These are the same emblems as Bhagwan Vishnu - she is the complete Shakti, encompassing all of creation's preserving force.
groups
Worshipped by All
Siddhidatri is worshipped not just by humans but by gods, asuras, gandharvas, yakshas and siddhas alike. Even Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva bow to her. She transcends all categories of being.
self_improvement
Shiva's Own Shakti
According to the Devi Bhagavata Purana, Shiva himself worshipped Siddhidatri at the beginning of creation to obtain his divine powers. From her grace, Shiva became Ardhanarishvara - half Shiva, half Shakti - the union of all.
02 - THE EIGHT SIDDHIS

The Eight Divine Perfections She Grants

The Markandey Purana lists eighteen siddhis. The most sacred eight - the Ashta Siddhis - are granted by Maa Siddhidatri to those who complete the Navratri vrata with sincere devotion.

01
Anima
The power to reduce oneself to the size of an atom - to become infinitely small.
02
Mahima
The power to expand oneself to infinite size - to become as vast as the cosmos.
03
Garima
The power to become infinitely heavy - immovable, unshakeable, beyond all force.
04
Laghima
The power to become infinitely light - to levitate, to move without friction through space.
05
Prapti
The power to obtain anything - to reach any place or object, to fulfil any desire instantly.
06
Prakamya
The power to realise all wishes - to make one's will irresistible in the fabric of reality.
07
Ishitva
Supreme lordship - the power to rule over all creation, nature and beings without resistance.
08
Vashitva
The power to bring all living beings under one's benevolent will - the power of divine love over all.
info

The greatest siddhi Maa Siddhidatri grants is not on this list - it is moksha, liberation from the cycle of birth and death. All eight siddhis are stepping stones; moksha is the destination. Sincere devotion to her on Day 9 is said to place the devotee on the path to final liberation.

सिद्धगन्धर्वयक्षाद्यैरसुरैरमरैरपि।
सेव्यमाना सदा भूयात् सिद्धिदा सिद्धिदायिनी॥

Siddha-gandharva-yakṣādyair asurair amarair api, Sevyamānā sadā bhūyāt siddhidā siddhidāyinī.

"Worshipped always by siddhas, gandharvas, yakshas, asuras and the immortal gods alike - may she, the bestower of siddhis, the Siddhidatri, be ever worshipped." This verse captures her universality - she is beyond all factions, beyond all distinctions. All beings seek her grace.

- Devi Mahatmya · Navratri Dhyana Shloka

03 - SHIVA'S WORSHIP

The Story: How Shiva Became Ardhanarishvara

self_improvement
ॐ

According to the Devi Bhagavata Purana, at the very beginning of creation - before the cosmos took its present form - Lord Shiva performed intense worship of Maa Siddhidatri. He wanted the divine perfections (siddhis) that would allow him to fulfil his cosmic role as the destroyer and regenerator of the universe.

Pleased by his devotion, Siddhidatri granted him all eight siddhis. But she gave him something even greater: she entered his very body - merging with him - transforming one half of Shiva's form into her own. From that moment, Shiva became Ardhanarishvara - half male, half female - the eternal union of Shiva and Shakti.

This origin story teaches that even the greatest of gods derives his power from the goddess. The universe is not sustained by masculine force alone - it is the union of Shiva and Shakti, stillness and energy, consciousness and creation, that sustains all that exists.

Chant 108 times on Day 9 to complete the Navratri journey. Her worship on Navami grants all eight siddhis and - for the sincere devotee who has completed all nine days - places them on the path to moksha.

05 - PUJA VIDHI

How to Worship Maa Siddhidatri on Day 9

01
Wear Purple or Violet

Purple and violet are the colours of spiritual completion and divine wisdom - the highest chakra colours. Wearing purple or violet on Navami honours Siddhidatri's supreme spiritual nature and the completion of the nine-day journey.

02
Offer Sesame Seeds and Halwa

Sesame seeds (til) and halwa are the traditional bhog on Navami. Sesame is associated with liberation and the dissolution of karma. Offering it on Day 9 is said to complete the spiritual work of the entire Navratri.

03
Kanjak Puja (if not done on Ashtami)

Many traditions perform Kanjak puja on Navami rather than Ashtami. Worship nine young girls as the nine Nav Durga forms, offer them food and gifts, and seek their blessings. This act of honouring the feminine divine in the form of a child is the most moving ritual of Navratri.

04
Complete Reading of Durga Saptashati

Navami is the day to complete the reading of the Durga Saptashati - all 700 verses of the Devi Mahatmya, begun on Day 1. Completing this sacred text in nine days is considered one of the most meritorious acts a devotee can perform during Navratri.

05
Visarjan and Thanksgiving

On Navami evening, the Navratri kalash (established on Day 1) is ceremonially immersed or respectfully closed. Offer gratitude to the goddess for the grace of the nine days. This visarjan is not a farewell - the goddess is everywhere always - it is the completion of a sacred contract of devotion.

06 - TEACHINGS

What Siddhidatri Teaches Us

local_florist

The Journey Itself is the Teaching

The nine forms of Nav Durga are not nine separate lessons - they are one continuous unfolding. To follow the series from Shailputri to Siddhidatri is to walk the complete path of the soul: from origin through devotion, battle, creation, love, victory, darkness and dawn - to fulfilment.

star

Siddhis Are Means, Not Ends

The eight siddhis she grants are extraordinary powers. Yet the greatest thing she grants is moksha - freedom from the cycle of becoming and un-becoming. Siddhidatri teaches that all powers, all attainments, all siddhis are instruments on the path to liberation, not destinations in themselves.

hub

Even Shiva Bows to Shakti

The universe's greatest destroyer, the most powerful of all beings, had to receive his power from the goddess. This is the final and most profound teaching of Nav Durga: all power flows from Shakti. The devotee who understands this is not diminished by it - they are liberated.

माँ सिद्धिदात्री - समस्त सिद्धियों और मोक्ष की दात्री

नवरात्रि के नवम दिन माँ सिद्धिदात्री की पूजा होती है। "सिद्धि" का अर्थ है दिव्य शक्ति और "दात्री" का अर्थ है देने वाली। ये कमल के आसन पर विराजमान हैं, चतुर्भुजी हैं - कमल, गदा, चक्र और शंख इनके हाथों में हैं। ये सिद्ध, गन्धर्व, यक्ष, असुर और देवताओं तक सभी द्वारा सेवित हैं।

देवी भागवत पुराण के अनुसार सृष्टि के आरम्भ में स्वयं भगवान शिव ने माँ सिद्धिदात्री की उपासना की थी। माँ की कृपा से उन्हें अणिमा, महिमा, गरिमा, लघिमा, प्राप्ति, प्राकाम्य, ईशित्व और वशित्व - ये आठों सिद्धियाँ प्राप्त हुईं। इसी कारण भगवान शिव का आधा शरीर देवी का रूप हो गया और वे अर्धनारीश्वर कहलाए।
